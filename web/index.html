StoryGraph to Goodreads

Convert your StoryGraph library export into the Goodreads CSV format that Fable and other apps can import. Everything happens in your browser; your file is never uploaded.

1Get your StoryGraph export

Open StoryGraph’s export page (you’ll need to be logged in), generate your library export, and download the CSV once StoryGraph emails you that it’s ready.
